A closer look at my current work

Building AI, learning from experiments, and making the data count.

At EXL, I work across Generative AI, Healthcare Campaign Analytics, and Data Science. The common thread is helping teams turn complex information into decisions they can trust.

01 GENERATIVE AI

Making complex information easier to explore

I build multi-agent analytics and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) solutions for healthcare campaign work. They help people ask questions of complex data and get grounded answers, instead of having to piece everything together by hand.

LLMsRAGMulti-agent workflowsHealthcare analytics

02 LLM QUALITY

Trust is built one answer at a time

A fluent answer is only useful if it is relevant and grounded. I work on evaluating and improving retrieval and response quality—raising Recall@5 from 68% to 81%, reducing hallucinations from 12% to 6%, and lowering query costs by 30%.
